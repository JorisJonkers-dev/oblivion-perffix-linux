# Known engine-level problems in Oblivion Remastered (2025, UE 5.3 over Gamebryo)

Research date: 2026-11-07. Method: web search and fetch, plus read-only use of the OblivionPerfFix repo (GitHub issues via `gh`, its CHANGELOG and README, and the local `docs/` copy). Nothing was benchmarked or run. No local file was changed except this one.

Legend for the status column: **Open** = not addressed by OblivionPerfFix (OPF). **Partly** = OPF or Bethesda changes part of it. **Fixed** = addressed by OPF (key named). **[UNVERIFIED]** = a single report, a forum claim, or a secondary paraphrase that the author could not check against a primary source.

## Limits of this research

- **Bethesda's own patch pages were not read.** `store.steampowered.com/news/app/2623190` returned navigation only. `elderscrolls.bethesda.net` was not fetched. The 1.1 and 1.2 notes below come from PureXbox's full-notes articles, read through a summariser and not verbatim. DSOGaming returned 403.
- **Nexus Mods pages returned 403.** Nexus rows are from search snippets only.
- **Reddit could not be searched.** The search tool blocks reddit.com, and the redlib mirror returned 403. No Reddit thread is cited.
- **The ProtonDB aggregate page could not be read.** Only user pages from search snippets are cited.
- **Digital Foundry's original PC and console articles** were not reachable. Their findings are cited through Notebookcheck, Spilled and PureXbox, which report them secondhand.
- **Hothardware and Eurogamer** were 403 or not found. Their claims are not used.
- **GitHub issue bodies and the CHANGELOG** were read directly through `gh` and the repo.

## 3. Notes

**Conflicts and open questions.**

- **Leak versus no leak.** DF and many Steam threads say performance falls with play time. The OPF team's 105-minute test found no drift, and it attributes object build-up to UE4SS Lua wrappers in its own harness (`docs/research.md`). The two could both be true, since the test route may not stress the same things. A repro is needed on a stock install (no UE4SS).
- **Traversal.** OPF says part of the traversal cost is fixed. DF and the 2026 Switch 2 article say the open-world stutter is still there on every platform. Both are consistent with a partial fix.
- **Mod regressions.** OPF issues #47 and #17 report regressions that the reporter links to OPF itself. They are not game bugs.
- **Switch 2 day-one patch.** One source says there was one. Another says there was none. Not resolved.

**Linux.**

- OPF installs as `version.dll` with `WINEDLLOVERRIDES="version=n,b" %command%` (`linux/README.md`). Nothing in the fix is Linux-specific.
- ProtonDB's aggregate page could not be read. The user snippets mention stutter on loading screens, world loading and menus, and input lag with some GPU and driver combinations [UNVERIFIED].
- Reddit could not be searched.

**Patch status to re-check.** Whether Bethesda's 2026 performance patch has shipped is unknown. Check the Steam news page directly before relying on this catalogue for 1.2.4 or later.

## 4. Summary: the eight not-yet-addressed issues that matter most to players

1. **M01 Long-session degradation (suspected leak).** Fps dips after an hour or more, cured by a restart. DF and many Steam posts report it; OPF's own test found no drift, so this is unresolved.
2. **C01 Crashes after 1 to 3 hours.** Random crashes to desktop in long sessions. No known trigger or cause; OPF guards only specific crash sites.
3. **C02 Crash minimised during start-up (issue #39).** Reproduces every time on one PC. Workaround: wait for the main menu before minimising.
4. **S04 Horse corral expensive state (issue #32).** 17-20 ms per frame after a jump onto the corral, where the game thread waits on the old engine.
5. **S06 Crowds and fights main-thread limit.** Frame drops in busy places, where the main thread is the limit.
6. **V02 Spell effect look stays on after the spell ends (issue #48).** Visible after the effect has ended, and reported by many players.
7. **V01 Dark-scene flash on menu open and close (issue #40).** Repeats every time a menu opens or closes at night.
8. **V03 Cheydinhal banners flip detail about 20 times a second (issue #41).** A flicker in a central town view; visibility not yet confirmed.

Not in the top eight, but still open: S01 cell-border traversal hitches (partly fixed), I01 input latency (anecdotal, unmeasured), and V06 cloud flicker and ghost docks (an OPF regression report).
