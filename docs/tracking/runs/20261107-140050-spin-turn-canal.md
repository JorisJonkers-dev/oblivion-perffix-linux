Luminance curve: exponential = monotone with a linear ln|L - final| fit (R2 >= 0.8; time constant of the fit: eye adaptation), monotone (time constant = time to 37 % of the gap), stepwise = two or more jumps (lighting / shadow pages), non-monotone, flat.
